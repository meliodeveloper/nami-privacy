sections:
  - heading: What it is for
    text: >-
      A broken boiler, a vet bill, a gap between jobs. Things you can't plan for
      but know will happen sometime. Without a pot, they usually go on a card or
      an overdraft.
  - heading: How big
    text: >-
      A common aim is three months of essential spending: rent, bills, food and
      getting to work. That can feel a long way off. Even a few hundred pounds
      covers most of the surprises that come up in a year.
  - heading: Where to keep it
    text: >-
      Somewhere you can reach in a day or two, separate from your spending
      account, so it is not spent by accident. An easy access savings account is
      the usual choice. It does not need to be the highest rate, just easy to get
      to.
  - heading: Is it safe
    text: >-
      Money in a UK bank or building society is protected by the FSCS up to
      £120,000 per person, per banking licence.
  - heading: Building it
    text: >-
      A small amount moved on payday, before you start spending, tends to work
      better than saving what is left at the end of the month.
