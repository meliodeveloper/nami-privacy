sections:
  - heading: Think in years
    text: >-
      £9.99 a month is about £120 a year. Looking at the yearly cost of each
      subscription makes it much clearer which ones you would buy again today.
  - heading: Free trials
    text: >-
      Most trials turn into a paid plan unless you cancel before they end. A note
      in your calendar on the day you sign up is the simplest guard.
  - heading: Where to cancel
    text: >-
      If you pay through the App Store, cancel in your iPhone's Settings under
      your name, then Subscriptions. If you pay the company directly, cancel in
      their app or account. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.
  - heading: If a company keeps taking money
    text: >-
      A payment taken from your card on a schedule is often a continuous payment
      authority. You can ask your bank to stop it, and they have to, even if the
      company says you must cancel with them first.
  - heading: Price rises
    text: >-
      Subscriptions often go up once a year. When one does, it is a good moment to
      ask whether you still use it enough.
