  - heading: Think in years
    text: |-
      A subscription is priced to feel small each month. Looking at the yearly cost makes it much clearer which ones you would still buy today.

      Here is a fairly ordinary set:

      - Video streaming: £10.99 a month
      - Music: £11.99 a month
      - Cloud storage: £2.99 a month
      - Gym: £30.00 a month
      - News app: £7.99 a month
      - **Total £63.96 a month, or £767.52 a year**

      Each line looks harmless on its own. The yearly total is what you are really deciding about.
  - heading: A ten minute check
    text: |-
      Once or twice a year, go through what you pay for:

      - List every subscription, including ones on a card you rarely use.
      - Next to each, write the yearly cost and when you last used it.
      - Cancel anything you would not sign up for again today.
      - For anything you keep, check there is not a cheaper plan you would be just as happy on.

      You can always rejoin something you miss. Most services make that easy.
  - heading: Monthly or yearly plans
    text: |-
      Yearly plans are often cheaper per month, but only if you keep using them for the whole year.

      For example, an app at £7.99 a month costs £95.88 a year. Its yearly plan at £59.99 saves about £36. But if you would have cancelled after four months, monthly would have cost £31.96.
  - heading: Free trials
    text: |-
      Most free trials become a paid plan unless you cancel before they end, and some charge for a full year at once.

      - Put a reminder in your calendar on the day you sign up, a couple of days before the trial ends.
      - Check whether the first payment is monthly or yearly.
      - Cancelling during a trial usually lets you keep using it until the trial ends.
  - heading: Where to cancel
    text: |-
      - **Paid through the App Store**: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
      - **Paid to the company directly**: cancel in their app, on their website, or by contacting them.
      - **Deleting the app does not cancel anything.**
  - heading: If a company keeps taking money
    text: |-
      A regular payment taken from your card is often a **continuous payment authority**. You can tell your bank to stop it, and the bank has to, even if the company says you must cancel with them first. If money was taken after you cancelled, you can ask your bank to get it back.

      For a Direct Debit, you can cancel it with your bank or in your banking app. You are covered by the Direct Debit Guarantee if a payment is taken in error.
  - heading: Price rises
    text: |-
      Subscriptions often go up once a year, and the notice is easy to miss. When one does, it is a good moment to ask whether you still use it enough, or whether a cheaper plan would do.
