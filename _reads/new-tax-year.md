sections:
  - heading: Your tax code
    text: >-
      HMRC may send a new code for the new year. Check the first payslip after 6
      April shows the code you expect.
  - heading: ISA allowance
    text: >-
      Each tax year you can put up to £20,000 into ISAs. Any allowance you don't
      use by 5 April is gone. From 6 April 2027, if you are under 65, no more than
      £12,000 of it can go into cash ISAs.
  - heading: Your P60
    text: >-
      Your employer has to give you a P60 by 31 May. It shows your pay and tax for
      the whole year. Keep it, you may need it to prove your income.
  - heading: Marriage Allowance
    text: >-
      If one of you earns less than the Personal Allowance and the other pays basic
      rate tax, you may be able to move part of the allowance across. You can
      backdate a claim up to four years.
  - heading: Self Assessment
    text: >-
      If you have income outside your job, such as self-employed work, your tax
      return for the year that just ended is due by 31 January.
