How publishing works

Published articles show on the site at once. In the app, a Read appears in Reads at the end of Insights (and as an About row on any screen you pick) the next time the app checks, within about an hour. An Explainer lives on the site only, for links from other articles. Every save is kept, so you can go back to any earlier version from History.
